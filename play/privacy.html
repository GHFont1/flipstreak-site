Privacy and support

Flip Streak collects no data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no in-app purchase. The game makes no network requests.

What stays on your device

To keep your progress, the game saves your streaks, flip history, statistics, daily challenge progress, achievements, unlocked coin designs, the two option names you last typed in Decide mode, and your settings. It is stored only in the app's local storage on this device. Deleting the app, or using Reset all progress in Settings, erases it.

A fair coin

Every flip is an independent 50/50 draw from the device's cryptographic random number generator. Nothing adjusts the odds. There is no betting, no wager and no currency of any kind: coin designs are unlocked only by playing.

Children

Flip Streak is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

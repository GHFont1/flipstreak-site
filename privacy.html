Privacy Policy

Flip Streak · Effective August 13, 2026

Flip Streak collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising identifier, and no third-party tracking code in the app.

What the app stores

Flip Streak keeps a small amount of game state so your progress survives closing the app: your best streak, the number of flips you have made, how many calls you got right, and how many landed heads versus tails. It also remembers whether you switched sound or haptics off.

All of it is written to your device's local app storage. None of it leaves your device, and none of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What the app does not do

  • No personal information is requested or collected
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier
  • No data is shared with or sold to third parties
  • No network requests are made by the game at all

Children

Flip Streak is suitable for all ages and is rated 4+. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

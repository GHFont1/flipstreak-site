Privacy Policy

Flip Streak · Effective October 1, 2026

Flip Streak collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising or advertising identifier, no in-app purchase and no third-party tracking code. The game makes no network requests.

What the app stores on your device

So your progress survives closing the app, Flip Streak saves your streaks, flip history and statistics, daily challenge progress, achievements, unlocked coin designs, the two option names you last typed in Decide mode, and your settings (sound, haptics, motion and appearance). All of it is kept in the app's local storage on your device. None of it leaves your device or is sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app, or using Reset all progress in Settings, erases it.

What the app does not do

  • No personal information is requested or collected
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising and no advertising identifier
  • No data is shared with or sold to third parties
  • No network requests are made by the game

Children

Flip Streak is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

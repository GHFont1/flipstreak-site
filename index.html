Flip Streak

Call it. Heads or tails. One miss ends the streak.

Flip Streak is a coin flip game with one rule that matters: keep calling it right. Every correct call adds to your streak. A single wrong call puts you back to zero. Your best streak is kept forever.

What is in it

  • A fair 50/50 flip, every time
  • Streak scoring with a permanent personal best
  • Milestones at 5, 10, 25, 50 and 100 in a row
  • Statistics: total flips, call accuracy, and the heads/tails split
  • Sound and haptics, both switchable

No strings

Free. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking, and nothing collected. Everything the game records stays on your device.

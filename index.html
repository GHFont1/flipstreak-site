Flip Streak

Call the face. Flick the coin. Land it.

Flip Streak is a physics trick-shot game played with a coin. Every target asks for a call, heads or tails. Drag back to set the angle and power, choose the spin, and let go. Where the coin lands and which face ends up on top come only from your throw, so every attempt teaches you something.

Play in your browser

What is inside

  • 24 hand-made levels on three tables: Kitchen Table (cups, shelves, bank shots), Game Room (springs, ramps, felt and steel) and Rooftop (fans and wind).
  • Three stars per level: land in the target, land the called face, fly through the ring.
  • Daily Shot: a new shot every day with five tries and a daily streak.
  • Streak Run: endless new targets that get harder. How many can you land in a row?
  • 12 coins that fly differently: sticky, bouncy, small, heavy, feather-light and more, unlocked by playing.
  • 28 achievements, stats, a short tutorial, and a coaching line after every shot that tells you what to change.
  • Decide: a small fair-coin tool for settling everyday choices.
A skill game, nothing to buy. No betting, no currency, no purchases, no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Works offline. For iPhone and iPad.

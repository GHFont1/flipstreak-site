How to play

Tap HEADS or TAILS to call the flip. A right call adds one to your streak; a wrong call sends it back to zero. Your best streak is kept. Milestones celebrate runs of 5, 10, 15 and more.

The latest version adds Round of 10 (ten calls and a score), Decide (type two options and flip to choose), three daily challenges with a daily streak, twelve coin designs to unlock, 27 achievements, and detailed statistics. Settings let you turn off sound or haptics, reduce motion, pick light or dark, and reset your progress.

Common questions

Is the coin actually fair?
Yes. Every flip is an independent 50/50 from the system's secure random number generator. Nothing adjusts the odds based on your streak, and a run of one side does not make the other side more likely.
Is there any betting or money involved?
No. There are no wagers, no currency, no prizes and no purchases. Coin designs are collectibles unlocked only by playing.
The coin landed on its edge. Is that a bug?
No. It is rare, it is real coin physics, and it never costs you your streak. Call again.
When do daily challenges reset?
At midnight on your device. Finish at least one challenge each day to keep your daily streak.
How do I start over?
Settings, then Reset all progress. Your sound and appearance settings are kept.
Does it work offline?
Yes, completely. It never connects to the internet.

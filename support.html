How to play

Tap HEADS or TAILS to call the flip. A correct call adds one to your streak. A wrong call sends the streak back to zero. Your best streak is kept permanently, and milestones arrive at 5, 10, 25, 50 and 100 in a row.

Common questions

Is the coin actually fair?
Yes. Every flip is an independent 50/50 draw from the system random number generator. Nothing adjusts the odds based on your streak, and a long run of one side does not make the other side more likely on the next flip.
Why did my streak reset?
One wrong call ends a streak. That is the whole game. Your best streak is never lost.
How do I turn off sound or vibration?
Tap the question mark in the top right for How to Play, which has switches for both. The speaker icon in the header also toggles sound directly.
How do I clear my statistics?
Open the chart icon in the top right and choose Reset statistics. It asks for confirmation, and it cannot be undone.
Will my streak sync to my other devices?
No. Everything is stored on the device it happened on, which is also why the app needs no account and collects nothing.
I reinstalled and lost my best streak.
Deleting the app removes its local storage, and there is no server copy to restore from.

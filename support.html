How to play

Each target shows a call: HEADS or TAILS. Drag back anywhere on the table, like a slingshot. The arrow and the dotted guide show the angle and power; let go to flick the coin. Use the minus and plus buttons or the slider under the table to change the spin. Land in the target with the called face up, and fly through the ring for the third star.

Daily Shot and Streak Run unlock after level 1-3. The Coin Purse lets you pick a coin with different physics for the shot.

Common questions

Is there any luck involved?
No. A shot is simulated with physics and has no random element: the same angle, power, spin and coin always land the same way. Only the small Decide tool uses a fair 50/50 coin.
How do I land the other face?
Keep the same throw and change the spin a little. After each shot a coaching line tells you roughly how much. Higher arcs stay in the air longer, so the same spin turns the coin more.
Is there any betting or money involved?
No. There are no wagers, no currency, no prizes and no purchases. Coins are unlocked only by playing.
The coin is standing on its edge. Is that a bug?
No. It is rare and real physics. It earns the landing star but not the call, because an edge is not a face.
When does the Daily Shot change?
At midnight on your device. Land the call on any of your five tries to keep your daily streak.
How do I start over?
Settings, then Reset all progress. Your sound and appearance settings are kept.
Does it work offline?
Yes, completely. It never connects to the internet.
